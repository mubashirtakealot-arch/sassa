SASSA SRD Status Check 2026 — Complete R370 Grant Guide

The SASSA SRD R370 grant is a lifeline for millions of South Africans. Checking your status regularly ensures you never miss a payment and can act quickly if something goes wrong. For the most complete and up to date guide visit SASSA Status Check 2026.

What is the SASSA SRD R370 Grant

The Social Relief of Distress grant pays R370 per month to South African citizens and permanent residents aged 18 to 59 who have no income and are not receiving any other government grant or UIF payment. The grant has been officially extended until 31 March 2027. To remain eligible your total bank deposits must stay below R624 per month including money received from family members. For full eligibility details visit SASSA Status Check.

How to Check Your SASSA SRD Status Online

The fastest way to check your SRD status is through the official SASSA portal at srd.sassa.gov.za. Enter your 13-digit South African ID number and your registered cellphone number. Your status will show for each month separately — you may be Approved for some months and Pending or Declined for others. For a full explanation of every status result visit SASSA Status Check 2026.

Check SASSA Status via WhatsApp

Save the number 082 046 8553 on your phone and send the word "Hi" on WhatsApp. SASSA's automated WhatsApp service will guide you through checking your status, payment date and application details. This service is available 24 hours a day and seven days a week. You must use the same cellphone number that is registered on your SASSA application. For more methods visit SASSA Status Check.

Check SASSA Status Without Data — USSD Code

If you have no data or internet access, dial the free USSD code by pressing star 134 star 7737 hash on your registered cellphone. This works on any South African mobile network including Vodacom, MTN, Cell C and Telkom and costs nothing. The USSD menu allows you to check your SRD status, view your payment date and check your appeal status without needing any internet connection. For all contact methods visit SASSA Payment Dates 2026.

What Does My SASSA Status Mean

Approved means you passed all eligibility checks and your payment will be processed. A payment date will appear on the portal within a few days. Pending means SASSA is still checking your details against Home Affairs, SARS, UIF and other government databases. Pending does not mean declined — check again in a few days. Declined means you failed at least one eligibility check — you have 90 days to appeal. Referred means SASSA needs to manually review your application and you should call 0800 60 10 11. How to Appeal a Declined SASSA Grant

If your SRD grant was declined you have the right to appeal within 90 days of the declined decision. Go to appeals.sassa.gov.za, enter your ID number and registered cellphone number, select the month that was declined and submit your reason for appeal. SASSA must respond within 90 days. If your appeal is approved you will receive back payments for all declined months. For the complete appeal guide visit SASSA Status Check.
